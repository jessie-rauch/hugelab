We cut the jargon.
Not the coverage.

Every insurer uses the same confusing terms. Here's what they actually mean, in plain English.

Deductible
The first bit you pay
Before we kick in, you cover a set amount each year. You pick how much. A lower deductible means a higher monthly cost, but less to pay when something happens.
Co-insurance
Your split after that
Once you've hit your deductible, we cover 90% of the remaining bill. You cover 10%. On a $2,000 visit, that's your deductible plus $200.
Pre-existing condition
Something already going on
If your pet was diagnosed with something before you enrolled, that condition won't be covered. Everything that happens after you join is covered. We'll show you exactly what applies.
Annual limit
The most they'll pay in a year
Most pet insurance caps what they'll pay out. We don't. A $40,000 surgery gets covered the same as a $400 one. You never hit a ceiling when it matters most.
