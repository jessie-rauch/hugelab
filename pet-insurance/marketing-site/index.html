How it actually works
01

You tell us about your pet. We tell you what’s likely ahead.

Different breeds get different things. A French Bulldog has different risks than a Golden. We’ve seen 25 years of claims — so we can actually show you what your specific pet is likely to need, and what it might cost, before you sign up for anything.

02

When something happens, we pay your vet. Not you, later.

Most pet insurance makes you pay the whole bill upfront and wait weeks to get reimbursed. We skip that part entirely. We pay your vet directly while you’re still at the clinic. You just cover your portion and go home.

💳
Concept illustration
Vet payment moment
Insurance words, translated
Deductible
The first bit you pay
Before we kick in, you cover a small amount each year. You pick how much — lower deductible means higher monthly cost, but less out of pocket when something happens.
Co-insurance
Your split after that
Once you’ve hit your deductible, we cover 90% of the remaining bill. You cover 10%. So for a $2,000 vet visit: you pay your deductible + 10% of the rest.
Pre-existing condition
Something already going on
If your pet was diagnosed with something before you enrolled, that specific thing won’t be covered. Everything that happens after you join? Covered. We’ll show you exactly what applies.
