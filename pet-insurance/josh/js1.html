The contrast with Emily
Six fields arrived pre-filled for Emily. One confirmed field arrives for Josh.
That's not a failure — it's the honest constraint of a Reddit referral link versus a ChatGPT structured handoff. The design argument here is different: what does a smart product do with almost nothing? It reads the signal, makes two careful inferences, and asks for the rest.

Why the greeting works
"Figuring out what a diagnosis costs" is exactly what he was doing.
Not "welcome to [Brand]." Not "ready to get a quote?" The opening line names his actual situation — which the app inferred from two URL parameters. That's the GenUI argument made concrete: the UI responds to what it knows, not to a template.
