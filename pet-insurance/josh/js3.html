The design challenge · Josh
Every other brand hid what wasn't covered. This one leads with it.
Josh walked away from three quotes because he couldn't get a straight answer on pre-existing conditions. This screen exists entirely to give him that answer before he pays anything. The hyperthyroid exclusion is named clearly — and so is everything the policy does cover. The honesty is the conversion mechanic.
Why the math block matters
Josh doesn't need a sales pitch. He needs the numbers.
He's already done the Reddit research. He knows methimazole is $30–40/month. What he can't calculate is his exposure to the next thing — kidney disease, cardiac, cancer — which senior cats face at elevated rates. The 3-year outlook gives him that frame without overselling it.
