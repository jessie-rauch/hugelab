The Wonder Weeks model
Anticipatory, not reactive.
This card surfaced the evening before Yoshi's recheck — not because Zach opened the app, not because he submitted a question. The product knew the appointment date from enrollment, knew the flagged condition from the first vet visit Zach logged, and surfaced the content before he knew he needed it. The dashboard context
A few weeks post-enrollment — not onboarding.
The register shift is deliberate: 6:47 PM, not 9:41 AM. Progress pips are gone. The bento gives the screen a settled, lived-in feel — coverage, claims, milestones, vet log. The proactive card is visually dominant not because it's a notification, but because something is actually happening tomorrow.
The per-pet architecture
Yoshi is the header, not a tile.
Every tile below is about Yoshi specifically — his coverage, his milestones, his vet history. When a second pet is added, the header becomes a switcher. The IA pattern is implied without needing to show it.
