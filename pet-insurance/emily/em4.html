The zero-claims reframe
$781 in. $0 claimed. That's the product working.
Emily knows she's paid nearly $800 and hasn't used it. The industry's failure here is treating that as neutral — sending a renewal invoice and hoping she doesn't do the math. This tile does the math for her, warmly: Daisy's been healthy, that's the goal, and the coverage is still there. Pre-renewal work without being a retention email.
